I'm Joshua Small, a software engineer in Charlotte, NC. I call myself an **accounting engineer**: most of my career has gone into the software that accounting firms and finance teams run on.

Since 2020 I've been a founding developer of accounting-firm software at Aiwyn, where I built the payments infrastructure from scratch: Stripe Payment Intents and Connect, ACH, Plaid, and international rails like Canadian PAD, BACS, and multi-currency. That platform grew from $24M to $2.38B in processed volume.

These days I spend my time where accounting systems meet applied AI: agents, evals, and the security and audit work that decides whether an AI system ever reaches production.

**Stack I know well:** Kotlin, Spring Boot, Java, Angular, TypeScript, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, GCP.
**Stack I'm building out:** Python, LLM evals, agents, MCP, RAG.

B.S. Computer Science, UNC Charlotte (cum laude).

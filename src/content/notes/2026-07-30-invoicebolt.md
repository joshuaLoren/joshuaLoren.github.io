---
title: A contractor just wanted to send an invoice
date: 2026-07-30
summary: Why I built Invoicebolt, a free invoice generator with no sign-up, no subscription, and no watermark.
---

A contractor here in town was just starting out. They'd done the work and needed to bill for it, and all they wanted was to make an invoice and send it.

Every tool they tried wanted an account first, or a subscription, or put its own branding on the PDF. That's a lot to ask of someone who has one invoice to send.

So I built [Invoicebolt](https://joshualoren.github.io/invoicebolt/). You fill in the form, and the browser makes the PDF. There's no account, nothing is stored on a server, and there's no watermark. The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/joshuaLoren/invoicebolt).

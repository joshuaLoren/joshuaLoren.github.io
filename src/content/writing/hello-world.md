I've spent most of my career building the plumbing under accounting software: billing, payments, the parts of a firm where money actually moves. Most of what I learned doing it never got written down anywhere outside a pull request.

This site is where that changes. Expect:

- **Write-ups** of systems I've built and the trade-offs behind them.
- **Notes** from learning the AI-engineering stack: evals, agents, retrieval, and making any of it safe enough to ship.
- **Projects**, live and in progress, with the source where I can share it.

If something here is useful, or wrong, I'd like to hear about it.
